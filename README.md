## SEO:

### Priority:

Developers should be pushed up in SEO.
This enables Search Engines to prioritize them over terms with similar names.
It also brings more eyes to them by allowing them to show up in searches for related terms.
In the unfortunate age of AI, SEO is crucial as people navigate the web less than they used to.
SEO is crucial for AI models to pick up on smaller developers.
